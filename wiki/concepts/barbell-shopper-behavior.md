# Barbell Shopper Behavior

## The Idea

A bifurcated grocery consumer base: middle-income households squeezed by
30%+ cumulative food price inflation since 2020 now split their spending
between discount and specialty retailers rather than shopping loyally at
one mid-market chain, increasingly relying on private-label products (now
21% of spending) and more frequent, smaller trips across multiple stores.

## Why It's Relevant

This directly bears on Meridian's stated goal of using its ~40,000-member
loyalty program to "understand our customer better." If Meridian's
loyalty members shop this way — splitting spend, trading into private
label, price-sensitive on some trips and experience-driven on others —
that reframes what "improve customer experience" and "increase revenue"
should mean in the dashboard's customer-facing analysis. The loyalty
purchase history itself can't go into AI tools, but this concept gives a
hypothesis to test locally against it.
