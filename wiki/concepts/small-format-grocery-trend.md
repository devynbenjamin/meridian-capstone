# Small-Format Grocery Trend

## The Idea

A national shift toward smaller-footprint grocery concepts —
micromarkets in the 3,000-5,400 sq ft range, sometimes bundled with added
services like EV charging — alongside more flexible, non-standardized
store layouts, as an alternative to conventional big-box grocery formats.

## Why It's Relevant

Meridian already positions itself partly on "a smaller footprint than
the national chains" (per the client brief), which may put it ahead of
or already aligned with this broader industry trend rather than needing
to catch up to it. Worth confirming in the interview where Meridian's
actual store sizes fall relative to this range, and whether store size
correlates with performance in Meridian's own data — a natural cut for
the dashboard, and directly relevant to sizing the Pasadena site.
