# Where Specialty Grocers Are Opening

Filed from a query on 2026-09-11: "What do our sources say about where
specialty grocers are opening?"

## What the Sources Say

Our sources describe this in terms of format and site type, not specific
geography:

- Specialty retailers (Trader Joe's, Whole Foods) are opening in
  grocery-anchored centers, which nationally run 4.0% vacancy vs. 6.3%
  for non-anchored properties and drew ~$11B in investment in 2025, up
  42% from 2024. — [JLL: Grocery Tracker 2026](../sources/jll-grocery-tracker-2026.md);
  the $11B/42% figure is corroborated in
  [ICSC: New Grocery Formats](../sources/icsc-new-grocery-formats.md).
- A specific format trend: small-format "micromarket" stores, 3,000-5,400
  sq ft, sometimes bundled with added services like EV charging (e.g.,
  Gelson's ReCharge). —
  [ICSC: New Grocery Formats](../sources/icsc-new-grocery-formats.md)
- Chains named as capturing this growth: Trader Joe's, Whole Foods
  ([ICSC](../sources/icsc-new-grocery-formats.md)), plus Sprouts
  ([JLL](../sources/jll-grocery-tracker-2026.md)).
- Geographically, JLL's data shows overall grocery expansion (not
  specialty-specific) concentrated in the Southeast, led by Florida, with
  secondary growth in the Mid-Atlantic. JLL itself flags this isn't about
  Meridian's LA/Orange/Ventura market. —
  [JLL: Grocery Tracker 2026](../sources/jll-grocery-tracker-2026.md)

**Gap:** neither source says where specialty grocers are opening
relative to Meridian's own market (Southern California) or relative to
underserved-neighborhood dynamics — that local/geographic angle isn't
covered by either article.

## Analysis — Not Stated in the Sources

Given Meridian's smaller-footprint positioning already resembles the
micromarket trend, and given Meridian's own expansion has been
opportunistic (leases opening up) rather than a formal site-selection
process (see [Lease-Driven Expansion](lease-driven-expansion.md)), it's
worth asking Dana directly whether Pasadena was chosen the same
lease-availability way, or whether it fits a more deliberate
specialty-format thesis like the one national players are following.
