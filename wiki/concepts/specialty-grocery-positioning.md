# Specialty Grocery Positioning

## The Idea

Competing against national grocery chains not on scale or price, but on
prepared foods, local sourcing, and a smaller-format footprint.

## Why It's Relevant

This positioning is the likely reason some of Meridian's stores succeed
in neighborhoods larger chains exited — it's the implicit hypothesis
behind the expansion strategy, including the Pasadena site. Whether this
positioning (versus, say, favorable lease terms or timing) is what's
actually driving performance matters for interpreting the store/category
dashboard results.
