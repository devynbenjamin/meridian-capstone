# Dana Okafor

## Who They Are

VP of Operations at Meridian Markets. Primary point of contact for this
engagement and author of the client brief.

## Why They Matter

She's the stakeholder the interview is being prepared for — her
priorities and communication style shape both what to ask and how to
reach her.

## What We Know

- Wants a dashboard for sales performance by store and category, aimed
  primarily at supporting the Pasadena expansion decision.
- Also interested in getting more value from the loyalty program data,
  which she says the company hasn't really used.
- Prefers email; travels Tuesdays and Wednesdays and is slow to reply —
  she's explicitly said not to read silence as a problem.
- Her assistant can schedule time but can't answer analytics questions —
  substantive questions should go to Dana directly.
- Wants something to show the board in about 3 weeks, even if
  preliminary, ahead of the full ~8-week engagement.

## Open Questions

- What counts as "preliminary but board-ready" to her — a wireframe, a
  couple of real numbers, something else?
- How involved is she personally in reviewing data/outputs versus
  delegating to her team (e.g., Marcus in IT)?
