# Meridian Markets

## Who They Are

A specialty grocery chain with 14 stores across Los Angeles, Orange, and
Ventura counties. Roughly $78M in annual revenue and ~620 employees.
Competes on prepared foods, local sourcing, and a smaller footprint than
national grocery chains.

## Why They Matter

This is the engagement's subject — the student team is building a
dashboard and analysis to help Meridian's leadership decide where to open
its next location.

## What We Know

- Grew from 6 to 14 stores in 5 years, largely by taking over leases from
  chains that exited neighborhoods Meridian considered underserved.
- Migrated to a new POS system last spring, viewed as an improvement.
- Runs a loyalty program (~40,000 members) that hasn't been meaningfully
  analyzed yet.
- Store performance has been uneven — some stores succeeded quickly,
  others have been slower to find their footing.
- Expansion and performance decisions have historically been made "on
  instinct and a spreadsheet," not data.

## Open Questions

- What structurally differentiates the faster-growing stores from the
  slower ones — location, size, timing — versus execution?
- How does Meridian define "underserved" when evaluating a neighborhood
  for a new site?
