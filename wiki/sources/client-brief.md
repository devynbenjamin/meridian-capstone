# Client Brief

Source: [client-brief.md](../../client-brief.md) (Dana Okafor, VP of Operations, August 2026)

## Key Takeaways

- Meridian Markets is a specialty grocery chain: 14 stores across Los
  Angeles, Orange, and Ventura counties, ~$78M annual revenue, ~620
  employees. Competes on prepared foods, local sourcing, and a smaller
  footprint than national chains.
- Grew from 6 to 14 stores in 5 years, largely by taking over leases from
  chains that pulled out of neighborhoods Meridian considered underserved.
- The ask: a dashboard showing sales performance by store and by category,
  to help leadership decide where to open the next location. Leadership
  already sees the Pasadena site as "the obvious next step" but wants data
  to back that up before committing.
- Growth has been uneven — some stores took off immediately, others have
  been slower — and expansion/performance calls have historically been
  made "on instinct and a spreadsheet."
- There's a loyalty program with ~40,000 members that Dana says hasn't
  really been used for analysis; understanding customers better is a
  secondary goal.
- Success criteria: increase revenue, reduce operating costs, improve
  customer experience — better visibility into store/category performance
  should support better decisions broadly.
- Data available: ~3 years of POS transactions, loyalty program membership
  and purchase history (~40,000 members), labor scheduling and hours, and
  store attributes (square footage, opening date, lease terms). Migrated
  to a new POS system last spring, seen as an improvement.
- Data access: IT (contact: Marcus) pulls an extract once the NDA is
  signed.
- **Data handling (firm, non-negotiable, covered by the NDA):** customer
  records and employee data — including loyalty program data, labor
  schedules, and any excerpts of them — never go into ChatGPT, Claude,
  Copilot, or any other AI tool. Sales totals by store and week, and store
  attributes, are fine to use with AI tools.
- Timeline: ~8 weeks overall; a preliminary version should be ready to
  show the board in about 3 weeks.
- Contact: Dana Okafor, VP of Operations. Email preferred; she travels
  Tuesdays/Wednesdays and is slow to reply (not a sign of a problem). Her
  assistant can schedule time but can't answer analytics questions.

## Open Questions

- What specifically makes the Pasadena site "the obvious next step" —
  demographics, an available lease, competitor presence, something else?
- What does "improve customer experience" mean operationally — faster
  checkout, assortment, loyalty perks, something else?
- Is there already an informal read on why some of the past expansions
  succeeded quickly and others didn't, beyond "instinct and a
  spreadsheet"?
- Is there a target or benchmark for what "good" store performance looks
  like, or is defining that part of this engagement?
- Does the loyalty data have enough geographic/behavioral detail to
  inform the Pasadena site question, or is it better suited to a separate
  customer-experience workstream?
