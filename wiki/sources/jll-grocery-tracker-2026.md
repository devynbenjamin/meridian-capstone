# JLL: Grocery Tracker 2026

Source: https://www.jll.com/en-ca/insights/market-perspectives/grocery-winners-are-built-for-value-fresh-format-and-convenience (JLL Market Perspectives)
Raw capture: [sources-raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026.md](../sources-raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026.md) (fetched summary, not full original text)

## Key Takeaways

- Confirms the value/specialty bifurcation seen in
  [ICSC: New Grocery Formats](icsc-new-grocery-formats.md), but frames it
  more sharply: "barbell shoppers" describes middle-income households
  squeezed by cumulative grocery price inflation exceeding 30% since
  2020, now shopping across multiple discount and specialty stores rather
  than one mid-market chain. Private-label products are now 21% of
  grocery spending.
- Value-focused growth: discounters (Aldi, Grocery Outlet) gaining
  budget-conscious shoppers.
- Fresh-format/specialty growth: premium, wellness-oriented chains
  (Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, Sprouts) capture affluent shoppers
  prioritizing experience and health over price — corroborates the
  specialty-positioning read from the ICSC source, and adds Sprouts as
  another specialty comparable.
- Real estate: grocery-anchored centers show 4.0% vacancy vs. 6.3% for
  non-anchored properties, commanding 4.4% NNN rent premiums. Investment
  volume ~$11B in 2025 (42% increase) — the same headline figure as the
  ICSC source, corroborated here. Institutional investor share reached
  27%.
- E-commerce: online grocery projected to reach $228B by 2030, with home
  delivery now outpacing pickup, reshaping store design requirements.
  (The brief doesn't mention Meridian having an online/delivery channel —
  worth asking about.)
- Geographic growth is concentrated in the Southeast (Florida leading)
  and Mid-Atlantic — not directly about Meridian's LA/Orange/Ventura
  market, included for context only.
- Canadian grocers (Loblaw, Empire, Metro) show the same discount-format
  expansion pattern, offered as cross-market confirmation of the trend.
